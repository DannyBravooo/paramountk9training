In-Home Training Services

Every dog is different. Each program is built around your dog's age, temperament, history, and the goals you have for life together.

Loose-Leash Walking

Teach your dog to walk calmly at your side instead of pulling and lunging. We practice on your own streets and sidewalks so better leash manners hold up on everyday walks.

Household Manners

Work through jumping, counter surfing, door rushing, overexcited greetings, and difficulty settling — in the exact rooms and situations where these habits happen.

Recall, Stay & Everyday Skills

Build reliable coming when called, sit, down, stay, and waiting at doorways, then proof those skills around real-world distractions in your neighborhood.

Positive-Reinforcement Training

We reward the behavior we want, communicate clearly, and stay consistent — no shock collars or harsh corrections. Training that builds confidence in your dog.

Puppy Foundations

Early socialization, crate and potty routines, name recognition, bite inhibition, and first obedience cues — a strong start while your puppy is most receptive.

Adult & Rescue Dog Obedience

Older dogs and rescues can absolutely learn new habits. We meet your dog where they are and build skills at a pace that fits their temperament and history.

Behavior Problem Solving

Address nuisance barking, jumping, leash reactivity, and other unwanted patterns with a structured, reward-based plan tailored to your dog.

Owner Coaching & Follow-Up

Handlers learn right alongside their dogs. You get clear instructions, hands-on practice, and continued support so progress sticks after the session ends.

New Dog & Rescue Settling-In Support

Just brought home a puppy or adopted a rescue? We help you set up routines, space, and expectations in the first critical weeks — crate and potty schedules, introductions to kids and other pets, and a calm plan for settling in. In-home guidance so the transition goes smoothly from day one.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions North Shore dog owners ask most often.

How old should my puppy be to start training?

Reach out before your puppy arrives or once they are home. We can discuss age-appropriate foundations and home routines. Ask your veterinarian about vaccination and exposure considerations.

Do you use shock collars or harsh corrections?

No. Paramount K9 Training uses positive reinforcement exclusively. We reward the behaviors we want, communicate clearly, and never use shock collars, prong collars, choke chains, or harsh corrections. Training should build confidence, not fear.

Do you train adult dogs or only puppies?

We train dogs of all ages. Adult dogs and rescues absolutely can learn new habits. We meet your dog where they are and build skills at a pace that fits their temperament and history. Many of our clients are adult dogs who need a manners refresher or help with specific behaviors.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Peabody and the surrounding North Shore communities, including Beverly, Salem, Danvers, Hamilton, Wenham, Topsfield, Boxford, and Middleton. Because we travel to you, your dog trains in their own home and neighborhood. Reach out if you're unsure whether your town is covered.

How many sessions will my dog need?

It depends on your goals and your dog. Our Starter package (3 sessions) covers foundations for puppies and basic manners. Core Obedience (6 sessions) builds reliable everyday skills. Total Transformation (12 sessions) is for complex behavior goals, reactivity, or multi-dog households. We'll recommend the right fit during your free consultation—no pressure, no upselling.

Do I need to be present during training?

Yes—owner coaching is a core part of our approach. We teach you to read your dog and reinforce the right behaviors, so progress continues between sessions. Training only works if the whole household is consistent, so we welcome family members to participate and learn together.

What should I have ready for the first session?

Have your dog's favorite treats, a flat collar or harness, a 6-foot leash, and any questions you want to discuss. We'll bring everything else. If your dog has any known behavioral triggers or medical considerations, let us know in advance so we can plan accordingly.
