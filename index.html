A little clarity

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Not sure where to start? Tell us what life with your dog looks like today.

How old should my puppy be to start training?

Reach out before bringing your puppy home or once they arrive. We can discuss age-appropriate foundations, home routines, and a suitable starting plan. Ask your veterinarian about vaccination and exposure considerations.

Do you use shock collars or harsh corrections?

No. Paramount K9 Training uses positive reinforcement exclusively. We reward the behaviors we want, communicate clearly, and never use shock collars, prong collars, choke chains, or harsh corrections. Training should build confidence, not fear.

Do you train adult dogs or only puppies?

Yes. We work with puppies, adult dogs, and rescues. Your dog’s history, temperament, and your household goals help shape the plan.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Peabody and the surrounding North Shore communities, including Beverly, Salem, Danvers, Hamilton, Wenham, Topsfield, Boxford, and Middleton. Because we travel to you, your dog trains in their own home and neighborhood. Reach out if you're unsure whether your town is covered.

How many sessions will my dog need?

Starter includes 3 sessions, Core Obedience includes 6, and Total Transformation includes 12. We’ll discuss your goals during a free consultation and recommend a starting point. Progress varies by dog and practice between visits.

Do I need to be present during training?

Yes. Owner coaching is part of the process. You will practice alongside your dog and leave with clear steps to work on between sessions. Family members are welcome to participate.

What should I have ready for the first session?

Have your dog's favorite treats, a flat collar or harness, a 6-foot leash, and any questions you want to discuss. We'll bring everything else. If your dog has any known behavioral triggers or medical considerations, let us know in advance so we can plan accordingly.
